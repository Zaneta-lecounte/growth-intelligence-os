# Growth Council Insight Brief

## Purpose
Create a concise cross-functional brief that converts fragmented growth signals into shared decisions.

## Audience
Representatives from areas such as:
- Growth / CRO
- Paid / Digital
- Email / Lifecycle
- Product Marketing
- Customer Marketing
- Partner Marketing
- Sales
- Marketing Ops / RevOps
- Product / Analytics when relevant

## Inputs
- new customer insights
- meaningful behavioral changes
- funnel performance changes
- experiment results
- sales feedback
- channel performance
- operational blockers

## Method
Use the sequence:

**Signal → Why it matters → Evidence → Hypothesis → Recommended action → Owner → Learning**
