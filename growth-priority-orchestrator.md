# Growth Priority Orchestrator

## Purpose
Combine outputs from all GIOS modules into a coherent growth roadmap.

## Inputs
May include:
- Growth Intelligence Diagnostic
- Customer Signal Synthesizer
- Behavioral Friction Analyzer
- Qualified Demand Leakage Auditor
- Experiment Opportunity Scorer
- Downstream Impact Analyzer
- open strategic priorities
- engineering / design capacity
- measurement readiness

## Method

### 1. Consolidate opportunities
Remove duplicate opportunities expressed by different teams.

### 2. Classify each opportunity
- customer problem
- acquisition
- conversion
- activation
- qualification
- retention
- revenue
- operations / measurement

### 3. Identify dependencies
Flag:
- data
- engineering
- design
- content
- sales
- product
- operations

### 4. Apply GROWTH scoring
Use the standardized scoring model.

### 5. Balance the portfolio
Avoid a roadmap made entirely of:
- quick wins
- large bets
- top-funnel tests

Recommended mix:
- foundational fixes
- high-confidence optimizations
- strategic experiments
- research / learning bets

### 6. Sequence
Suggested horizons:
- Now
- Next
- Later
