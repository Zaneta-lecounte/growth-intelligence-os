# Growth Intelligence Diagnostic

## Purpose
Identify the most important growth problem, determine where value is being lost, and recommend the next best action using multiple signal types.

## Use when
- conversion or pipeline performance declines
- a channel produces volume but weak downstream quality
- a team knows something is wrong but does not know why
- multiple teams have partial evidence about the same problem

## Inputs
- business goal and target metric
- funnel data
- acquisition/source data
- behavioral analytics
- customer research / VoC
- sales or customer-facing feedback
- current experiments or recent changes
- downstream revenue / quality data
- known instrumentation limitations

## Method

### Step 1 — Define the business signal
State:
- what is underperforming
- compared with what baseline
- for which audience / segment
- over what period

Do not diagnose before the business signal is clear.

### Step 2 — Classify supporting signals
Organize evidence into:
- Customer signal
- Behavioral signal
- Funnel signal
- Acquisition signal
- Operational signal
- Revenue / quality signal

### Step 3 — Separate facts from interpretation
For every important observation, label it:
- Observed
- Inferred
- Unknown

### Step 4 — Identify the leakage point
Find the stage or interaction where the largest meaningful loss occurs.

### Step 5 — Generate plausible causes
Create 2–5 root-cause hypotheses.

Each hypothesis must state:
- suspected cause
- supporting evidence
- contradictory evidence
- missing evidence
- confidence level

### Step 6 — Choose the next best action
Select one:
- experiment
- additional research
- analytics/instrumentation fix
- messaging change
- journey redesign
- targeting change
- process / handoff change

### Step 7 — Define success
Include:
- primary metric
- guardrail metric
- downstream metric
- expected learning
