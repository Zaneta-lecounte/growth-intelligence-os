# Customer Signal Synthesizer

## Purpose
Turn qualitative customer evidence into structured growth intelligence without flattening nuance or overstating frequency.

## Inputs
Any combination of:
- customer interviews
- surveys
- chat transcripts
- sales call notes
- support tickets
- win/loss notes
- reviews
- open-text form responses

## Method

### 1. Normalize the evidence
For each input, capture:
- source
- customer / prospect segment
- journey stage
- verbatim or close paraphrase
- topic
- sentiment
- business relevance

### 2. Cluster by customer problem
Group evidence into themes such as:
- unclear value
- trust / proof
- pricing uncertainty
- implementation risk
- feature fit
- switching cost
- complexity
- urgency
- competitive comparison

### 3. Distinguish frequency from importance
Do not assume a frequent comment is automatically the most important.

Score each theme:
- frequency: 1–5
- severity: 1–5
- commercial relevance: 1–5
- journey relevance: 1–5

### 4. Connect themes to behavior
For each theme, identify:
- expected behavioral manifestation
- funnel stage likely affected
- analytics signal that could validate it

### 5. Convert themes into testable questions
Example:
Customer signal: "I cannot tell what is included."
Testable question: Does clearer package framing improve qualified conversion without reducing lead quality?
